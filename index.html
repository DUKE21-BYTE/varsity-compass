Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers on KUCCPS, funding, and university transition.

How does KUCCPS placement work? +

KUCCPS operates an automated, merit-based system. It assesses you based on your KCSE mean grade and specific cluster subjects required for your chosen degree or diploma. We provide strategic selection strategies so you do not miss out on competitive courses.

Can I change courses after placement (IIT)? +

Yes, through the Inter-Institutional Transfer (IIT) process. It requires endorsement from your current institution and the one you wish to join. We guide students through the complex portal paperwork to ensure a successful switch.

What are the challenges of High School to University transition? +

Most students face challenges with academic independence, managing finances (HEB loans), and securing affordable hostels. We offer Campus Transition counseling to prepare students for the social, academic, and financial realities ahead.

How do I correct errors on my HEF application? +

Mistakes in data entry can put you in the wrong funding band (e.g. Band 5 instead of Band 2). We assist in error correction and writing appeal letters to the Higher Education Funding board.
